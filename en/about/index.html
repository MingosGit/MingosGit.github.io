The short story

I combine two worlds that don't always go together: the technical side — Python, SQL, Power BI, deep learning — and the language of business — finance, KPIs, decision making. That mix comes from studying Computer Engineering with an Economics minor, and it's the common thread in all my projects.

Before my degree I completed a Higher Vocational Diploma in Systems Administration with a cybersecurity track, and at AXA I worked in critical corporate support, handling incidents for more than 4,000 users of a global insurer. That job taught me something you don't learn in class: how to talk to business users and turn what they ask for into concrete actions.

In July 2026 I joined Coty as a DRP Intern in the Barcelona Supply Chain team. A month earlier I took part in InterHack BCN with the Damm challenge — optimising delivery routes and truck loading with 83,000 delivery lines — my first contact with operations data at that scale.

In my free time I build projects that force me to learn something new: business intelligence systems, business simulators with automatic optimisation and games with AI. They're all published in this portfolio, with the code on GitHub.
